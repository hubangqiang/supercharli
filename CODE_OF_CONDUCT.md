# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive collaboration environment.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and professional.
- Discuss ideas, not personal traits.
- Provide actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent while staying precise.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, insults, or discrimination.
- Doxxing, intimidation, or threats.
- Repeated bad-faith disruption.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject content that violates this code.
Repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent bans.
